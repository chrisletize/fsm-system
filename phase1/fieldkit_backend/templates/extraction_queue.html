One work order, one lifecycle — an extraction job is never cloned into a new WO each day. The same WO stays "Extraction Active" from day one through pickup; the daily log and per-day equipment billing all accrue onto that single WO.

Retrieved is per piece of equipment. Tick only the units actually coming back — if some areas are still wet, leave that equipment unticked and it stays deployed and keeps accruing days. Each unit's billed days are computed from its own deploy-to-pickup span, so a fan left behind three extra days bills three extra days. Use Retrieved all equipment when the whole job comes back at once.

The job only flips to Completed once the last unit is picked up — a partial retrieval keeps it active in this queue.

Escalated (the highlighted row) just means the job has been active longer than the configured warning threshold — it doesn't block anything, it's a flag to go check on it.
