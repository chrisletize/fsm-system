Delinquent means a customer has an open invoice more than 90 days past its invoice date (Chris's chosen threshold, not the industry-default 60) — it's the same flag shown as a red badge on customer detail, the WO form, and the dispatch board, all computed by the same nightly job so this page can never disagree with those badges.

No Contact flags a customer with zero contacts marked "accepts billing" — they'll be silently skipped by batch statement runs and Record Payment has nowhere to send a receipt until a billing contact is added.

Portal filters to customers whose active compliance-portal enrollment is marked as their primary billing method (see the Compliance page) — these are invoiced through the portal rather than emailed directly.

Record Payment only offers a customer's real open invoices with a balance — it can't apply money to something already paid or voided.
