Draft → Sent → Approved/Declined → Converted. Only a Draft estimate can be edited — once Sent, the customer has a PDF copy with a frozen tax rate/total, so the line items lock the same way an invoice does once Hardened.

Approve/Decline only apply to a Sent estimate. Declining requires a reason, kept for the record.

Convert only appears once Approved — it opens a new work order pre-filled from the estimate's lines, site, and contact. The estimate itself flips to Converted the moment that work order is actually saved (not when you click Convert) and links to it — so an abandoned WO form never leaves an estimate in a half-converted state.
